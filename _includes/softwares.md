## Softwares

- [NeuralSAT](https://github.com/dynaroars/neuralsat): A neural network verification tool based on the DPLL(T) SMT Solving algorithm. 
- [Abstract Domain Explainer](https://hocdot.github.io/abstract-domain-explainer/): An interactive walkthrough of how DNN abstract domains work.
- [Lean the Easy Way](https://hocdot.github.io/lean-the-easy-way/): An interactive course that teaches you to read Lean and audit AI-generated proofs.
- [CSConfs](https://cspicks.roars.dev/csconfs): A schedule for Computer Science conferences
